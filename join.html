The Five Pillars

Five ways we build connection.

01
Move

Workouts, runs, and movement classes. Friendship through doing the hard thing together.

02
Play

Pickleball, game nights, and tournaments. Easy, regular, ongoing.

03
Talk

Dinners, salons, and conversations that matter. Long tables, real questions.

04
Social

Open hangs, parties, and member nights. The default plan for a Friday.

05
Serve

Volunteer days and community impact work. Chicago is the third member of every friendship here.
