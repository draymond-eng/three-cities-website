What it is

A clubhouse, a calendar, a circle of people.

01 · The Space

Two Chicago clubhouses.

Daily access to work, hang, or host. River North and Wicker Park, open every day for members.

02 · The Calendar

Programming every week.

Workouts, dinners, talks, and trips designed for real connection. Something on every day of the week.

03 · The People

350+ members.

A community of adults who actually show up. Across industries, neighborhoods, and stages of life in Chicago.

The Five Pillars

Five ways we build connection.

01
Move

Workouts, runs, and movement classes. Friendship through doing the hard thing together.

02
Play

Pickleball, game nights, and tournaments. Easy, regular, ongoing.

03
Talk

Dinners, salons, and conversations that matter. Long tables, real questions.

04
Social

Open hangs, parties, and member nights. The default plan for a Friday.

05
Serve

Volunteer days and community impact work. Chicago is the third member of every friendship here.
