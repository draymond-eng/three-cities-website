Networking Events in Chicago

Networking events in Chicago, without the networking.

Most networking events in Chicago feel like trading business cards. Ours feel like dinner with people you are glad you met.

Apply for Membership → See the Calendar

Three Cities Social is a Chicago social club with two clubhouses, in River North and Wicker Park. A good share of what we do is, on paper, networking. Dinners where you meet someone in a field you have always been curious about. Talks where the person next to you turns out to run the thing you have been reading about. Member nights where the only agenda is the room.

We just do not run it like networking. No name tags, no elevator pitches, no working the room. The connections happen because the evening is good, not because anyone is trying to make them happen. That is the whole idea behind a social club built for adults who want real friends and useful people in their corner.

The Formats

Where the real introductions happen.

i.
Member dinners

Long tables, a dozen seats, one host. You sit next to a stranger and leave with two new people worth knowing. The most reliable networking event in Chicago that never calls itself one.

ii.
Talks and lunches

Founders, operators, writers, and makers, talking about the work and taking real questions. Community events in River North and Wicker Park, built for people who want substance over small talk.

iii.
Member nights

Open hangs, happy hours, and parties across both clubhouses. The low-pressure way to meet people in Chicago, week after week, until the room starts to feel like yours.

iv.
Move and play

Run club, pickleball, beach volleyball, game nights. Doing something together beats standing around a high-top. Some of the strongest connections here started over a scorecard.
