    What your membership covers.

    Four ways in. The three full plans share the same access, both clubhouses and every program. Community is events only. The lower the rate, the longer you commit.

    We review every application to keep the community strong. Most decisions come back inside a week.

    • i.

      Daily access to both clubhouses

      River North and Wicker Park. Open every day for members to work, hang, eat, or host.

    • ii.

      Most weekly programming

      Dinners, runs, talks, pickleball, volunteer days, member nights. A small number of partner events carry an outside venue or instructor fee, called out on the calendar.

    • iii.

      Two guests at any time

      Bring two people with you to the clubhouses or any open program, at no cost.

    • iv.

      Host your own gatherings

      Book a dining table, the boardroom, or the terrace for your own dinner, meeting, or get-together.

    • v.

      Member directory and intros

      Find members who do what you want to do. We will make the introduction.

    • vi.

      Reciprocal access

      As we open in more cities, your membership travels with you.
