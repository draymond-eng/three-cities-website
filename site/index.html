What it is

A clubhouse, a calendar, a circle of people.

i.

Part social club, part coworking.

The clubhouses stay open around the clock to gather, host, or get work done. It works like a coworking social club, where the people at the next table are members you will actually get to know.

ii.

Where members meet people in Chicago.

Dinners, talks, run club, volunteer days, and member nights. The kind of calendar that turns into actual friendships, not more contacts.

iii.

Membership, by application.

Social club membership in Chicago, reviewed by application. One rate, both clubhouses, every program on the calendar.

Five Pillars

Five ways we build connection.

i.
Move

Runs, workouts, and movement classes. Doing hard things together is the fastest way to actually know somebody.

ii.
Play

Pickleball, game nights, ping pong tournaments, pool table standoffs. Friendship in low stakes.

iii.
Talk

Dinners, talks, and conversations that go past small talk. Long tables, real questions, no phones.

iv.
Social

Member nights, parties, open hangs. The default plan for a Friday, hosted by people you know.

v.
Serve

Volunteer days and community work. Chicago is the third member of every friendship here.
